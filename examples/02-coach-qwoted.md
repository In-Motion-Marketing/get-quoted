# Example 2: Coach mode (Qwoted)

An illustrative session. The expert and details are made up.

Qwoted bans AI-written pitches, so Claude does not write the pitch. It interviews, then hands back the expert's own words.

---

**Priya:** Help me answer this Qwoted request: "Seeking HR leaders on how small companies should handle their first layoff. Looking for practical steps."

**Claude:** This fits your profile: HR operations, 9 years, including two restructures at a 60-person company.

Qwoted's rules ban pitches written with AI tools, and it runs AI detection. So I'll work in **coach mode**: I'll ask you some questions, give your own words back in order, and you write the pitch. Breaking the rule can get your account suspended.

1. What's the first thing a small company gets wrong?
2. What did you actually do in your restructures?
3. What would you tell a founder never to do?

**Priya:** They tell people on a Friday afternoon by email. We did it face to face, on a Tuesday morning, with the severance letter already printed and a named person each employee could call that week. Never let managers wing the conversation. We scripted the first two minutes.

**Claude:** Two angles: (A) how to tell people, using your Tuesday process, or (B) why managers need a script. I'd go with A, since the request asks for practical steps. Which one?

**Priya:** A.

**Claude:** Here are your words, in a suggested order. Qwoted suggests 7 to 12 short sentences, so you have room to add a step or two.

1. **Answer first:** "They tell people on a Friday afternoon by email."
2. **What you did:** "We did it face to face, on a Tuesday morning, with the severance letter already printed and a named person each employee could call that week."
3. **The blunt don't:** "Never let managers wing the conversation."
4. **The detail that makes it real:** "We scripted the first two minutes."

Write the pitch in your own words, using these as notes. Share your draft if you want feedback.

**Priya:** *(shares a draft)*

**Claude:** Notes, not rewrites:

- Your opener is strong. Keep it
- The third sentence repeats the first. Cut it
- Say how many people were affected, if you're comfortable sharing that. A number makes it quotable
- You haven't mentioned the "named person to call." That's your most practical step

**Priya:** *(revises it and pastes it into Qwoted)*

---

**What to notice:** Claude never wrote a sentence of the pitch. Its feedback was notes, not replacement text, and it told the user why it was working that way.
