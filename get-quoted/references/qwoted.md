# Qwoted

Last checked: November 2026. Pricing and rules change, so confirm on the site.

- **Site:** https://www.qwoted.com. Opportunities live at https://app.qwoted.com/source_requests
- **Best for:** expert commentary, product recommendations, speakers, and podcast guests. Region filter covers the US and UK
- **Cost:** free Basic plan allows 2 pitches a month, with a 2-hour delay before the expert can pitch. Paid plans raise the limit. Credits reset monthly and do not roll over. Some opportunities need no credit ("No Pitch Credit Needed" filter)
- **How queries arrive:** a daily "Request Roundup" email at 2 PM local time, real-time topic alerts, and the in-app feed

## AI rule: Coach mode only

Qwoted's Terms and Community Guidelines ban pitches written with AI tools. It runs AI detection plus human moderators, reporters can flag pitches, and flags can lead to suspension or removal. Qwoted warns that even AI grammar fixes can trip detection.

On Qwoted, always use coach mode (see SKILL.md): interview, hand back the expert's own words in order, and let them write the pitch. Tell the user why.

## Answer format

- No published character limit. Qwoted suggests 7 to 12 sentences, with bullets and short sentences
- The expert's name and title come from their profile, so leave them out of the pitch. If the profile's Employment section is empty, the pitch shows as "an Expert from Qwoted"
- Files up to 20 MB can be attached

## Submitting

- Pitches go in the on-site answer box only. Do not contact reporters outside Qwoted unless they ask
- Unlocking an opportunity spends a credit even if the expert never pitches, so pick questions before unlocking anything
- Pitches can be edited for 24 hours. No follow-ups until the reporter replies

## Qwoted's own tips

- Pitch within 12 hours
- Answer every question in the request up front
- Give something that can't be Googled: firsthand numbers, client stories
- Write one quotable line. Don't restate the question, and don't sell
