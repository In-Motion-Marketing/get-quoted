# Source of Sources (SOS)

Last checked: November 2026. Confirm rules on the site.

- **Site:** https://www.sourceofsources.com. Free list run by Peter Shankman, who founded the original HARO
- **Cost:** free
- **How queries arrive:** email, up to three times a day. Each query lists the journalist's name, email, outlet, and deadline. No dashboard and no category filter, so expect off-topic queries in every email

## A strict relevance rule

SOS says it will ban anyone who pitches off topic, even once, with no appeals. Its own test before sending: is this truly helpful, relevant, timely, and accurate? If it is not a confident yes, don't send.

On SOS:

- Raise the bar in Step 1. Only shortlist queries squarely inside the expert's topics. When in doubt, recommend skipping and say why
- No sales language, no product mentions unless the query asks
- SOS's guidance discourages AI-written pitches, and one bad reply can end the account. Use coach mode: interview, hand back the expert's own words in order, and let them write the email

## Answer format

- No published length limit. Use the default: short, about 4 sentences
- Nothing is attached automatically. End with the expert's name, title, company, and one link, plus a subject line that names the query
- Respect the stated deadline

## Submitting

The expert replies to the journalist by email, from their own inbox. In coach mode (see SKILL.md), the expert writes the email. Never send it yourself. The user sends from their own inbox.

SOS's terms ban scraping its emails by any automated means, including AI. Work only from queries the user pastes in or forwards to you, one at a time, not from bulk-processing the list.
