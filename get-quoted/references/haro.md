# HARO (Help a Reporter Out)

Last checked: November 2026. Confirm rules on the site.

- **Site:** https://www.helpareporter.com. Sources sign up at /sources
- **Owner:** Featured, which also runs Connectively, where the same queries appear earlier. HARO relaunched in April 2025
- **Best for:** every beat, from national staff reporters to freelancers, bloggers, and podcasters
- **Cost:** free for sources, paid for by ads in the newsletter
- **How queries arrive:** three emails each weekday, at about 5:35 a.m., 12:35 p.m., and 5:35 p.m. US Eastern time. Each query has a deadline, which is the hard stop

## Rules

- No off-topic pitches, and no product pitches unless the query asks for one. Breaking the rules can mean a lifetime ban
- Every pitch gets an AI score, and many journalists filter out pitches flagged as fully AI-written. Keep it in the expert's own words
- No harvesting reporter details

## Answer format

- No published length limit. Follow the query, otherwise use the default: short, about 4 sentences
- HARO does not attach a profile. End with a short bio line (name, title, company) and contact details
- Attachments are stripped. Share supporting material as a link

## Submitting

The expert replies by email to the masked @helpareporter.net address in the query. Draft the subject and body. Never send it yourself. The user sends from their own inbox.
