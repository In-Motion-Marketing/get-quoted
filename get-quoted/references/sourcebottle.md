# SourceBottle

Last checked: November 2026. Confirm pricing and rules on the site.

- **Site:** https://www.sourcebottle.com. Call-outs by category at https://www.sourcebottle.com/industry-list.asp. Responding needs a login
- **Best for:** experts in Australia, New Zealand, the UK and Ireland, Canada, the US, and Singapore. Strong on business, health, lifestyle, parenting, property, tech, and travel. Many posters are bloggers, podcasters, and content creators, not just journalists. Each call-out is labeled Journalist, PR Pro, or Content Creator
- **Cost:** answering call-outs is free on every plan. Paid plans add SourceBottle staff pitching the expert to journalists
- **AI rules:** none published (unconfirmed)
- **How queries arrive:** "Drink Up!" emails, up to two per weekday at 10am and 2pm in the expert's chosen timezone, filtered by topic and country

## Before shortlisting

Check each call-out for:

- **Poster type.** Journalist, PR Pro, or Content Creator. Tell the user which, since a brand's content request is not press coverage
- **Local-only.** Many call-outs require sources in a specific country
- **Paid or unpaid.** Never ask for payment unless the call-out offers it
- **Deadline.** Call-outs often close early once the poster has enough sources, so work these first

## Rules

A reply that does not answer the call-out counts as spam. SourceBottle's terms say spammers can be banned immediately and may be named publicly. No harvesting emails.

## Answer format

- No site-wide length limit. Each call-out sets its own, for example "1 to 2 sentences plus name, title, headshot." Follow it exactly
- Otherwise use the default: short, about 4 sentences
- Include credentials and contact details. SourceBottle advises stating your credentials and your link to the topic, and giving one or two quotable insights with a clear opinion
- Don't reply with a question, and don't send "happy to help" with no substance

## Submitting

The expert responds through SourceBottle while logged in, or by the email or phone method the poster chose. Draft the text for the expert to paste in.

SourceBottle's own tip: reply within about 30 minutes when you can, because posters stop reading once they have enough sources.
