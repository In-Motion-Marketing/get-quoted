# Connectively

Last checked: November 2026. Pricing and rules change, so confirm on the site.

- **Site:** https://www.connectively.us. Questions at https://www.connectively.us/experts/questions. The user signs in themselves
- **Owner:** Featured, which also runs HARO. Featured moved its expert Q&A platform to Connectively in June 2026
- **Best for:** experts who want quotes on online publisher sites across many topics. HARO queries also appear here, ahead of the HARO email
- **Cost:** the free plan allows 3 answers a month. Paid plans raise the limit. Respect the user's monthly cap when shortlisting
- **How queries arrive:** the dashboard, keyword alerts, and a daily round-up email. An "Opportunities" tab lists requests from Substack, X, LinkedIn, and elsewhere. Those are answered at the original source, not on Connectively

## Answer format

- Aim for around 64 words, roughly 420 characters, and no more than 4 sentences
- Allowed range is 100 to 2,500 characters. Stay near the bottom of it
- The platform attaches the expert's name, title, company, and LinkedIn. Leave out greetings, sign-offs, and contact details
- Write the quote exactly as it should appear in print. No headers or bullets unless the question asks
- No self-promotion and no placeholders like "Company A"

## AI and originality rules

- Every answer gets an AI likelihood score, and publishers see it. Some questions are marked as rejecting AI-written answers. Check before drafting, and use coach mode (see SKILL.md) on those
- Plagiarism checks reject reused quotes, including the expert's own past answers. Every answer must be new
- Answers outside the expertise on the expert's profile get ranked lower
- Experts who keep asking publishers for edits get warned, then banned

## Submit by hand

Connectively tags answers submitted through workflow tools or agents as "Automated," and publishers can hide every expert with that tag. The user pastes and submits each answer themselves. Never fill in or submit the answer form.

## Finding questions

The user browses the queue and pastes or shares the questions they want to look at. Do not browse or click around the logged-in site yourself.

Search tip for the user: the filter box matches from the start of the question text, so search the opening words of a question, not a word from the middle.

## Connectively's own tips

Share firsthand experience, specifics, and a bold take. "If you have to Google the question," skip it.
