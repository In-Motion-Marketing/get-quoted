# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (November 2026)

First public release.

- Expertise profile with proof for each topic, saved between sessions
- Fit test and skip rules, with a hard stop on off-topic answers where one strike can mean a ban
- Interview, specificity gate, angle options, and short drafts in the expert's words
- Coach mode for platforms that ban AI-written answers
- Platform notes, with sources, for Connectively, HARO, Featured, Qwoted, Source of Sources, MentionMatch, SourceBottle, ResponseSource, and #journorequest
- Safety rules for untrusted query text, scams, and confidential information
- The user submits every answer. The skill never sends, posts, or follows up
- Three example sessions
